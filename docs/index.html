Engineered for Security

Built Different from Third-Party Apps

Most follower apps ask for your Instagram password, send your data to obscure servers, and get your account suspended. Who Unfollowed You operates entirely inside your own browser.

No Password Required

The extension uses your active, logged-in browser session cookies directly. Your password is never asked, stored, or processed.

100% Local Storage

All snapshot comparisons and history are saved strictly in your device's local browser storage. Zero tracking pixels, zero analytics.

Dual-Key Identity Matching

Matches accounts by both permanent numeric ID and username. If a follower changes their username handle, they will never be falsely flagged as an unfollower.

Incomplete Fetch Protection

If Instagram throttles a scan mid-pagination, the algorithm suppresses false unfollow accusations for unfetched users and protects your history.

Deep Relationship Insights

Filter instantly between people who don't follow you back, followers you haven't followed back (fans), and mutual connections with clean search.

Data Export & Cleanup

Export all historical snapshots as structured JSON anytime, or delete any tracked username data permanently with a single click.

Simple Setup

Ready in Less than 60 Seconds

No external accounts to create, no verification codes to input, and no complicated setup.

Step 01

Install the Extension

Add the extension to Chrome, Brave, Edge, or any Chromium-compatible browser. Pin the magnifying glass icon to your toolbar.

Step 02

Open Instagram

Log in to your regular Instagram account on instagram.com in any browser tab. The extension securely accesses the active web session.

Step 03

Run Scan & View Results

Click "Scan Data". In seconds, your dashboard compares your current list with previous snapshots to pinpoint every unfollower.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can this extension get my Instagram account banned?
No. Unlike mobile apps that log into your account from strange server IP addresses, this extension runs directly inside your own browser using your existing browser session and standard Instagram web endpoints, with built-in rate-limiting delays and backoff timers.
Does the extension require my password?
Never. The extension does not have password fields, never prompts for login credentials, and cannot read your passwords. It relies solely on your authenticated browser cookies on instagram.com.
Where is my follower data stored?
All data is stored exclusively on your local device using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API. Nothing is transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party analytics services.
How does it detect unfollowers if this is my first scan?
On your first scan, the extension saves an initial snapshot of all your followers and following. From your second scan onward, it computes the exact diff between snapshots to identify who unfollowed you, who followed you, and who doesn't follow back.
