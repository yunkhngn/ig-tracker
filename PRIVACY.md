# Privacy Policy for Who Unfollowed You for Instagram™

*Last updated: October 28, 2026*

**Who Unfollowed You for Instagram™** ("we", "our", or "the extension") is an independent browser extension committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

---

### 1. Data Collection and Transmission
- **No Remote Data Collection:** We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your personal data, credentials, cookies, or account lists to any remote server or third party.
- **No External Analytics:** The extension contains zero tracking pixels, telemetry, or remote analytics scripts. All fonts and assets are packaged locally within the extension.

### 2. How Data is Used and Stored
- **Local Browser Storage Only:** All scans, follower snapshots, and history are stored exclusively on your device using Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.local` API.
- **Authentication:** The extension relies solely on your existing, active session cookies within your own browser to retrieve your follower/following lists. Your password or login credentials are never accessed, requested, or stored.
- **Data Deletion:** You can delete any saved account data at any time directly through the extension's Dashboard ("Xoá dữ liệu"), or by removing the extension from your browser.

### 3. Permissions Explanation
The extension requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:
- **`storage` & `unlimitedStorage`:** To save your follower snapshots locally so you can compare changes between scans.
- **Host Permissions (`https://*.instagram.com/*`):** To read follower and following lists directly from your authenticated browser session.
